Range Rules

The club establishes the following regulations for safe and responsible range use:

Flag and Firing Line Requirements

Range flag at gate must be raised anytime you are on the property. Range flag at firing line must be raised when shooting on the line or downrange. Rifle shooting must occur exclusively from designated firing lines.

Safety and Conduct Standards

Members must adhere to all safe handling guidelines and bear responsibility for guest behavior. The club prohibits artificial lighting during shooting. Eye and ear protection is required at all matches.

Firearm Restrictions

Center fire rifles, including .223 pistols, may not be fired toward or in pistol ranges #1 and #2. Shotgun use, hunting, and binary explosives are completely banned on the property.

Target and Range Management

Shooters must take all targets and trash they brought to the range home, and shoot only at appropriate targets, avoiding items that create ricochets. Live ammunition should be disposed of in designated containers.

Operational Policies

Members must follow the club calendar. A member must accompany guests at all times. No alcohol is permitted. Vehicles may access backstops on designated rock surfaces only.

Membership Terms

Annual dues of $150 are due by September 10th. New members complete mandatory range orientation before receiving membership cards.
