Membership Info

Membership Rules & Agreement

Download Rules & Agreement (PDF)

Membership dues are $150 annually, due August 1st of each year. Failure to pay dues by September 10th will result in the termination of your membership.

Members must be current members of NRA and show proof of unexpired NRA membership. All new members will be required to have a range orientation before membership is approved. Contact the following to schedule orientation.

  • Sheldon Peacock — (620) 282-8554
  • Dennis Kephart — (620) 617-3053

Due to a limited number of memberships, Kiowa Gun Club is currently not accepting new memberships. If you wish to be placed on a waiting list please email your request to kiowa369@outlook.com.
